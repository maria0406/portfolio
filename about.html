Maria Cruz

ECE and Plan II Honors junior at UT Austin, working toward product and program management.

I grew up in Houston, Texas, and got into technology through high school robotics, where I learned to solve complex problems with limited resources and to collaborate under pressure. I'm a first-generation college student and an Amazon Future Engineer. I also founded Space City Tech, which runs free, hands-on STEM programs for underserved communities in Houston. In 2023, Ashoka named it one of 16 finalists in the T-Mobile Changemaker Challenge.

My curiosity about technology led me to electrical and computer engineering at UT Austin, where I've spent the past few years building skills across full-stack development, embedded systems and machine learning. I'm also in Plan II Honors, and taking engineering alongside its liberal arts courses has given me a well-rounded, holistic education.
